## 🛠️ Key Responsibilities & Tasks

### 🔹 1. IT Service Management (ITSM) & SLA Monitoring

Supported daily IT service monitoring activities to help ensure functional incident tickets were tracked and followed up according to established service targets.

**Key Activities:**

- Reviewed daily **SLA monitoring reports** for unresolved functional incident tickets.
- Monitored reported SLA compliance levels, including periods showing **96% compliance**.
- Identified overdue, approaching-due, and unresolved incidents requiring attention.
- Followed up with respective teams to support incident closure within the established **0–3 day SLA target**.
- Reviewed ticket statuses, due dates, and outstanding actions.
- Supported operational monitoring through consistent reporting and follow-up.

**Knowledge & Skills Developed:**

- IT Service Management (ITSM)
- SLA compliance monitoring
- Incident lifecycle awareness
- Operational performance tracking
- Issue escalation and follow-up coordination

### 🔹 2. Incident & Problem Ticket Management

Assisted in monitoring functional problem tickets associated with enterprise application operations.

**Key Activities:**

- Reviewed weekly **Functional Problem Ticket Reports**.
- Monitored ticket statuses and resolution progress across **BCRM coordinator groups**.
- Reviewed reports containing approximately **17–22 problem tickets per week** during the documented reporting periods.
- Identified tickets requiring status updates or further follow-up.
- Tracked SLA due dates and outstanding issues to support operational visibility.
- Maintained awareness of unresolved functional issues affecting system operations.

**Knowledge & Skills Developed:**

- Incident and problem tracking
- Ticket status monitoring
- SLA deadline management
- Issue reporting and documentation
- Coordination with technical teams

### 🔹 3. Change Request (CR) Management

Supported Change Request monitoring and reporting activities by maintaining structured tracking records.

**Key Activities:**

- Reviewed and maintained the **Approved Change Request (CR) Tracker** using Microsoft Excel.
- Updated CR statuses, implementation timelines, and progress remarks.
- Assisted in maintaining the **CR Master List** for centralized monitoring.
- Reviewed ongoing Change Requests to identify missing or incomplete information.
- Prepared summarized tracking information, including:
  - Variation Request (VR) reference numbers
  - Related enterprise systems
  - Change descriptions
  - Assigned Persons-in-Charge (PICs)
  - Current progress and outstanding information
- Supported documentation and follow-up activities relating to pending Change Requests.

**Knowledge & Skills Developed:**

- Change Request tracking
- Change management process awareness
- Data organization and validation
- Excel-based operational reporting
- Project status monitoring

### 🔹 4. IT Project Monitoring & Deliverables Tracking

Assisted in preparing and maintaining project deliverables timelines to support enterprise IT project monitoring.

**Key Activities:**

- Updated project delivery timelines using **Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint**.
- Monitored planned deliverables and project activities across multiple enterprise systems:
  - **CSCRM**
  - **FICA**
  - **Business Intelligence (BI)**
  - **Data Management (DM)**
- Organized project activities into structured timelines for management discussions.
- Updated presentation materials to communicate delivery progress and upcoming activities.
- Supported monitoring of milestones, outstanding deliverables, and project schedules.
- Assisted in maintaining consistent and accurate project reporting records.

**Knowledge & Skills Developed:**

- Project timeline management
- Project deliverables tracking
- Progress reporting
- Data presentation and visualization
- IT project coordination

### 🔹 5. User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Exposure

Gained practical exposure to enterprise application testing through observation of User Acceptance Testing activities.

**Key Activities:**

- Attended and observed **User Acceptance Testing (UAT)** activities at **Menara TNB Melaka (MITC)**.
- Observed interactions between business users and project stakeholders during testing activities.
- Gained an understanding of how UAT supports business requirement validation.
- Developed awareness of application testing within the system delivery lifecycle.

**Knowledge & Skills Developed:**

- User Acceptance Testing (UAT) fundamentals
- Business requirement validation awareness
- Application testing workflows
- Enterprise system delivery processes
- Stakeholder involvement in software testing

### 🔹 6. Solution Review & Stakeholder Coordination

Supported project communication and coordination through regular participation in solution review and internal project meetings.

**Key Activities:**

- Attended regular **Solution Review Meetings** to understand system development progress, outstanding issues, and proposed solutions.
- Prepared and refined presentation materials for:

- Reviewed project updates, unresolved issues, and upcoming action items.
- Prepared meeting action logs documenting:

  - Assigned action items

  - Target completion dates
- Supported follow-up activities by maintaining organized meeting and project documentation.

**Knowledge & Skills Developed:**

- Stakeholder communication
- Meeting documentation
- Action item tracking
- Cross-functional coordination
- Technical presentation preparation

Participated in enterprise-level discussions relating to business process alignment, resource management, and IT service delivery responsibilities.

**Key Activities:**

- Gained exposure to work scope realignment between **Rapid Delivery (RD)** and **Digital Enterprise (DE)**.

- Enterprise IT operational workflows
